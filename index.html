The Blue Wave Story

More than a stay,
a memory

Nestled along British Columbia's rugged west coast, Hotel Blue Wave is a sanctuary where the rhythm of the ocean sets the pace of life.

For over two decades we have curated experiences that blend warm Canadian hospitality with refined coastal luxury.

  • Award-winning Wellness spa & relaxation centre
  • Three oceanfront dining venues
  • PADI certified dive school on-site
  • Intimate wedding & private coastal events
