Who We Are

Born from a love
of this coastline

Hotel Blue Wave was founded in 2001 by the Laurent family, who grew up along this very stretch of BC's coastline. What began as a small guesthouse with eight rooms and a deep appreciation for West Coast hospitality has grown into one of Canada's most charming boutique coastal retreats.

We are not a chain hotel. We are a place shaped by people who genuinely love this land — its flavours, its rhythms, its warmth. Every decision we make, from the food on your plate to the thread count of your linen, is made with that love in mind.

Today, 214 team members — most of them from the local community — carry forward a tradition of care that no five-star rating can fully capture.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

🤝

Genuine Hospitality

We believe warmth cannot be scripted. Our team is trained to listen, anticipate and respond with sincerity — because every guest has a different story.

🌿

Sustainability First

Solar-powered, ocean-conscious, locally sourced. We are committed to protecting the coastline that gives us everything — for future generations to enjoy.

🎨

Artisan Excellence

From handcrafted furniture to chef-curated menus, we partner with local craftspeople and producers who share our obsession with quality.

👪

Community Roots

Over 80% of our team hails from individuals from coastal communities across British Columbia. We invest in training, education and fair wages as a cornerstone of our identity.

✨

Quiet Luxury

No noise. No spectacle. Just exceptional comfort, considered design and the unhurried pace of life by the ocean — refined without being pretentious.

🔄

Continuous Growth

We never stop learning. Regular guest feedback, global hospitality trends and a restless curiosity push us to improve the experience every single year.

Our Journey

Two decades of milestones

2001

A Family Dream Opens Its Doors

The Laurent family opens Hotel Blue Wave with 8 rooms and a beachfront restaurant. Bookings come almost entirely by word of mouth.

2006

First Major Expansion

As touris grows along British Columbia's coastline, the hotel expands to 40 rooms and introduces a wellness spa inspired by West Coast natural therapies.

2011

National Recognition

Hotel Blue Wave is recognized among Canada's top boutique coastal hotels. Guest satisfaction reaches new heights, with high occupancy throughout the year.

2016

The Beach Villas Launch

Six private beach villas with infinity plunge pools open, each designed by acclaimed local architect George Ryle using sustainable materials.

2020

Going Carbon Neutral

Blue Wave becomes the first hotel on Canada's western coast to achieve verified carbon neutrality, powered by a 480-panel solar array.

2025

Today — and Beyond

86 rooms, 214 team members, and a new underwater coral restoration programme. The family is still involved. The spirit hasn't changed.
